About a month ago I got a random idea when a friend said they have bunch of fake nails and pain and UV curing machine. 
  
It's something that often only women wear, so what if we could turn it into an accessory that cna be used by men too? 
Only issue that men often dont want something unpractical- that makes using their hands kinda limited.
  
My idea was that we could make tiny simple FFC PCBs that have a coil on it. This coil could harvest energy from NFC and power forexample a LEDs, this is by itself a super cool to show off - 
like in dark lighting up nails as they move. But this is just a gimmick. 
  
So what if we place tiny metal probes on pcb, so at the tip of the nail it can be used as a way to probe into electricla circuits without having dedicated probes in hand, 
so if you have to do a lot of manual dialing of other devices you dont have to have to pick up and let down the probes. 
Possibly even have signal generator connected. 
The connection would be done via having thin wires connected from nails to a watch-like board on wrist, where the wires are turned into standard connectors to be connected with Multimeters, 
Osciloscopes, Signal generator. 
Probably two probes per hand are most feasible, using the thumb for ground, and using the pointer and middle finger for all other functions. 
  
Another idea was a health aspect, we could embed specific materials on nails that specifically react to UVA light and UVB light, this is becuase these damage the skin a lot and 
being able to detect if theres too much of this light around could warn the person wearing that skin damage is happening. The method of warning is fluorescence.
Combining these into a pack that is then put on medium long nails( to accomodate probes, or the nfc coil) is something that both genders would find it useful. This could make a real 
practical usecase why to have fake nails.
  
I wonder what do you think of these ideas, would you be interested in such products? do you have any other ideas?
