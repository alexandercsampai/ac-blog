A friend invited me intviteded me into Győr recently, for a housewarming party, I haven't really been to this town before, I possibly went trough it but that's it.
So I wanted to explore the town a little bit, wondered how similar could be to Brno, since it's a smaller town but in similar size category.
Didn't expected much since it's Hungary.
I havent went much out of town just walking around, overall its very pretty town, calming without much traffic, easily accessible with by foot. 
The nature is beutiful, plenty trees, wide roads.
But haven't tested public transportation - didn't need to, the town is small and in half an hour you can kinda reach any part of it. 
some building do give off communistic vibes - maybe sometimes too many of them.
  
I wanted to go to some library just to study a little but sadly I got the realization that not many are open saturday afternoon. 
First Library I went to had on Google maps that its open till 16 on saturdays.
Well since then they updated the place and only till 13.. sent an update request with new photo to google maps, kinda felt misslead but its fine.
The second Library is a University library, is open till 16 too, Just its paid for anyone external. There's no option for an external person to come in even if i have ISIC.
But this lead to a nice place next to it, It's called "Irodalmi kávézó". 
It's litterally next to the library, they're open till 17 which is perfect. 
The best part: I'm absolutely alone in the whole place, nobody talking nobody annoying, i can just be in peace here without worrying about anything. Quite cozy
  
Thank you for reading my blog post! Have a nice day<3
